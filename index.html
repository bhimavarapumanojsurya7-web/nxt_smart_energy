The Challenge

The Shared Electricity Dilemma

Hostels and PGs often use one main electricity meter for multiple AC rooms. This makes it difficult to calculate each room’s electricity usage and can cause billing confusion, disputes, and electricity wastage.

Single Master Meter Dilemma

When 15 to 50 air conditioners run on a single utility meter, building managers cannot identify who consumed how many units of electricity.

Unfair Flat-Rate Splitting

Frequent Billing Disputes

Tenants who use their AC for 2 hours a day are forced to pay the same electricity bill as tenants who run theirs 18 hours daily, leading to constant friction.

Tenant & Warden Arguments

Rampant Electricity Wastage

Without individual accountability, ACs are routinely left on while rooms are empty, driving electricity bills to exorbitant commercial slabs.

20% to 30% Power Wasted

Conventional Shared Meter

  • ✕ One lump-sum utility bill split equally among all rooms
  • ✕ Zero visibility into individual AC kilowatt-hour usage
  • ✕ High risk of unpaid bills and delayed tenant deposits
  • ✕ Tenants leave AC running continuously with no consequences

With NXT Smart Sub-Meter

  • ✓ Every tenant pays strictly for their room's actual AC consumption
  • ✓ Live TFT screen and IoT portal display exact kWh and Rupees
  • ✓ Prepaid billing ensures owners never suffer unpaid power costs
  • ✓ Tenants turn AC off responsibly, lowering total energy consumption
The Innovation

Our Solution

An IoT-Based Prepaid Smart AC Energy Sub-Meter engineered to track individual AC electricity consumption, calculate exact costs, support prepaid billing, and provide owners with effortless digital monitoring.

Individual AC Circuit Sub-Metering

Mounted directly on the AC power circuit line of each room to monitor high-draw cooling loads accurately without interfering with standard light/fan wiring.

Automated Prepaid Deduction

Tenants purchase prepaid electricity units in advance. As the air conditioner runs, units deduct in real time according to the building's set rate per kWh.

Dual Display: On-Device TFT & IoT Web

Tenants see their remaining rupee balance right on the wall screen, while property owners track building-wide energy consumption on a central web interface.

Operation Workflow

How It Works

A simple, safe, 5-step operational architecture from electrical connection to intelligent cloud management.

1

Step 1: Connect the meter to the AC electrical circuit

The NXT sub-meter is securely wired into the dedicated power line supplying the room's air conditioner, ensuring safe isolation.

Hardware Installation • Dedicated Line
2

Step 2: Measure electricity consumption using the energy sensor

The integrated sensor module samples alternating current (AC) and voltage at high frequencies to calculate exact real-time power draw and kilowatt-hours.

PZEM-004T Module • Precision Current Transformer
3

Step 3: Process readings using the ESP32 controller

The onboard dual-core ESP32 microcontroller computes instantaneous costs, deducts units from prepaid credit balance, and manages relay safety logic.

ESP32 Core • Edge Computing
4

Step 4: Display energy usage and estimated cost

The on-device color TFT display refreshes continuously, presenting the tenant with their live balance in Rupees, units used, and power status.

Color TFT Display • Room-Level Visibility
5

Step 5: Let the owner monitor usage through the IoT interface and manage prepaid usage

The building owner views all rooms simultaneously via the IoT web interface, tops up prepaid credits upon payment, and reviews historical usage logs.

IoT Cloud Interface • Remote Management
Hardware & Software Architecture

Technology Used

Built using robust, accessible, and industrial-proven embedded hardware components tailored for Indian electrical conditions.

ESP32 Microcontroller

Core Processor

High-performance 32-bit dual-core microcontroller with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Handles sensor polling, cost calculation algorithms, and secure IoT telemetry.

Architecture Xtensa Dual-Core 240MHz
Connectivity 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi + BLE

PZEM-004T Module

Energy Measurement

Specialized electrical parameters measuring module capable of reading voltage (80–260VAC), AC current (up to 100A), active power, power factor, and cumulative energy.

Measurement Range 80-260V / 0-100A AC
Interface TTL Serial UART

TFT Display

On-Device Screen

Crisp SPI color digital display showing live electrical stats, room identifier, rupee balance, and warning notifications without needing a mobile phone.

Display Type Color SPI TFT LCD
Interface High-Speed SPI Bus

Current Sensor & Relay

Sensing & Cut-off

High-precision non-invasive current transformer for safety, coupled with a heavy-duty 30A switching relay designed to safely disconnect power on balance exhaustion.

Relay Rating 30A 250VAC Switching
Safety Isolation Optocoupler Isolated

Cloud & Web Software

Secure web-based dashboard and API service for owner administrative controls, room-by-room balance allocation, usage analytics, and alert notifications.

Protocol MQTT / HTTP REST
